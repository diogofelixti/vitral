# Code of conduct

Vitral adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: treat everyone with respect, criticise ideas and code, never people, and accept that
the maintainers may remove comments, commits and contributions that fall short of that.

It applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and every other project space.

## Reporting

Open an issue describing what happened. If it should not be public, open an issue asking for
private contact, without details, or write to the maintainer through the contact on their
GitHub profile. Every report is read, and no one who reports is retaliated against.
